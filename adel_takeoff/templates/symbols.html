Symbol library

The company library is shared by every job in this data folder. Starter symbols are drawn for this tool. Box one symbol on a sheet, or pick one from the library, and set the similarity cutoff. Each match is a traceable item with its score and confidence. A score below the high cutoff stays in review until you accept it. A mark on the plan that is missing from the legend is listed on the review page so it can be sent as an RFI.
