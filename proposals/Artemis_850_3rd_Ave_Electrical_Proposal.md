Taken off the July 17, 2026 landlord-review PDFs only. Fixture counts are one per type tag on E-200 and E-201. Fire-alarm counts are plan-area text labels on FA-100 and FA-101. The architectural fixture schedule and a legible MDB schedule were not in the set.

Distribution read from E-400 and the power plans: existing 600 A disconnect remains; new splice in the engineer’s office; 2 sets of 4#350 kcmil in two 2-1/2 in. conduits; LP-C and LP-1 are 400 A MLO, 120/208 V, 65 kA, 60-pole, 200% neutral; KP-C and KP-1 are 100 A MLO with 3-pole 30 A shunt-trip hood feeds. LP-1 demand printed on the schedule is 72.25 kVA (200.56 A at 208 V).

## 3. Scope

Division 01 covers coordination, submittals, BEC and fire-alarm filing labor, testing, and as-builts. Agency fees are reimbursable.

Division 26 covers the feeder (80 LF included), MDB, four panels, branch devices, cellar and first-floor mechanical connections listed on E-100 and E-101, DWH-1 at 3-pole 200 A, hanging of the 317 fixtures, 24 emergency battery fixtures, and Lutron rough-in for two hub locations and 25 placeholder lighting circuits. Security is empty raceway only. Shades are excluded.

Division 28 covers a new tenant FACP on the line side of the tenant panel, Class B FPLP wiring, devices counted below, alarm/trouble/supervisory to the existing lobby fire command station, two telco pairs to the demarc, programming, and the acceptance test.

Cellar fire alarm labels: 14 F, 2 H, 6 SL, 4 CO with strobe, 5 MM1, 1 MM2, 2 SD, 3 R, 3 WP, 1 TS, 1 WF, 1 FACP.

First-floor fire alarm labels: 14 F, 3 H, 1 SL, 7 CO with strobe, 4 MM1, 1 MM2, 4 SD, 6 R, 3 D, 3 MH, 1 TS, 1 WF, 1 RA.
